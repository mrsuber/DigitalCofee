1. Introduction

Welcome to Digital Coffee ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and services.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and authentication credentials
  • Profile Data: User preferences, settings, and profile information
  • Authentication Data: When using Google or Apple Sign-In, we receive limited profile information

2.2 Usage Data

  • Session Data: Listening sessions, duration, completion status, and wave type preferences
  • Progress Metrics: Total sessions, total minutes, daily streaks, and longest streaks
  • Analytics: Alpha vs Beta wave usage distribution and session history
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, and app version

2.3 Feedback Data

  • Feedback messages, ratings, and support requests you submit through the app

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • Service Delivery: Provide and maintain the Digital Coffee app and its features
  • Personalization: Track your progress, build streaks, and customize your experience
  • Analytics: Understand how users interact with our app to improve features
  • Communication: Send important updates, notifications, and respond to support requests
  • Security: Protect against unauthorized access and fraudulent activity
  • Legal Compliance: Comply with applicable laws and regulations

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Firebase Services

We use Google Firebase for:

  • Firebase Authentication: Secure user authentication and account management
  • Cloud Firestore: Store user data, sessions, and progress metrics
  • Firebase Storage: Store and deliver audio files
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: Send push notifications (with your permission)

4.2 Security Measures

  • Industry-standard encryption for data in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Encryption at rest for stored data
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Access controls and authentication mechanisms

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do NOT sell your personal data. We may share your information only in these circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Firebase (Google), payment processors, and analytics services
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our legal rights
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets
  • With Your Consent: When you explicitly authorize us to share your data

6. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Export: Download your data in a portable format
  • Opt-Out: Disable push notifications or email communications
  • Withdraw Consent: Revoke permissions at any time

7. Children's Privacy

Digital Coffee is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected data from a child, please contact us immediately.

8. Cookies and Tracking

  • Preferences: Remember your settings and preferences
